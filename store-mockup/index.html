About this game

ONE SHIP. ONE COMMANDER. EIGHTEEN STAR SYSTEMS.

Cosmic Commando is a top-down space combat game set in a scaled, real solar system and the stars around it. You fly the Commander, humanity's newest fighter, from Earth orbit to the alien-held stars beyond the jump gates.

ANSWER THE MAYDAY

Battle groups call for help across human space. Fight beside carriers, battleships and destroyers while thirty allied fighters launch around you, and hold the line until the hospital ship arrives.

TAKE THE JOB, LAUNCH

Dock at orbital stations for station defence, escorts, convoys, rescues and bounty hunts on named alien Overseers. Spend your credits on ten tiers of shields, hull, engines and weapons.

REAL STARS, REAL DISTANCES

Jump through the gates to Alpha Centauri, Tau Ceti, Sirius and TRAPPIST-1. Each system has its own planets, stations, nebulas, asteroid belts and wrecks to salvage.
